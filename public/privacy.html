Privacy

Updated October 8, 2026.

What this preview stores

The Idea board saves in your browser's local storage. It is separate from your account dashboard and is not synced. You can remove local ideas from the board or clear site storage.

This design-feedback build has no login, account creation or cloud idea dashboard. The separate protected account preview is not opened by this link.

Early-access requests

The request form stores your email privately in Supabase for TrendPulse to contact you about early access. It creates no account and sends no automatic email. Public visitors cannot read the list. Request removal by emailing founders@officialtrendpulse.in.

Public sources and hosting

Google Trends India supplies public topic data and source images. Loading an external image or following a source link connects you to that provider. Google Fonts supplies fonts. Vercel hosts this site and may process request and security logs.

Your choices

Delete local ideas from the Idea board or clear this site's browser storage. For early-access email removal or other data requests, contact founders@officialtrendpulse.in.

Do not enter passwords, payment information or other sensitive data in idea fields. This preview collects no payments.

Contact

Questions or requests: founders@officialtrendpulse.in.
