Privacy

Updated October 8, 2026.

What this preview stores

The workshop idea board saves in your browser's local storage. It is separate from your account dashboard and is not synced. You can remove workshop ideas from the board or clear site storage.

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email, authentication details and saved account ideas. Google sign-in requests basic account identity. We do not ask for access to your Gmail or Google Drive. Authentication email is sent using Resend.

Early-access requests

The request form stores your email privately in Supabase for TrendPulse to contact you about early access. It creates no account and sends no automatic email. Public visitors cannot read the list. Request removal by emailing founders@officialtrendpulse.in.

Public sources and hosting

Google Trends India supplies public topic data and source images. Loading an external image or following a source link connects you to that provider. Google Fonts supplies fonts. Vercel hosts this site and may process request and security logs.

Your choices

Sign out on shared devices. Account sign-in is stored in this tab's session storage. Export displayed account ideas from the dashboard; the view contains up to 100 recent ideas. Delete individual ideas using the dashboard. For account deletion or other data requests, email founders@officialtrendpulse.in. Automated full-account deletion and full-history export are not offered in this preview.

Do not enter passwords, payment information or other sensitive data in idea fields. This preview collects no payments.

Contact

Questions or requests: founders@officialtrendpulse.in.
